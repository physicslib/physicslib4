## Status of previous findings

All three findings from `review-2026-09-sec10.5.md` are **fixed**, verified by reading:

- **H1 (Einstein causality / local von Neumann algebras proved about the wrong
  ambient algebra) — fixed.** `HaagKastlerNet.commAlgebra` and
  `CovariantQuasilocalAlgebra` are gone (`grep -rn "commAlgebra\|CovariantQuasilocalAlgebra"`
  across `Physicslib4/` returns nothing; the two tex hits at line 480 are historical
  narrative about the removal, not stale links). `LocalCommutativity` still
  quantifies existentially over *some* `QuasilocalAlgebra U i`
  (`LocalCommutativity.lean:74-81`), but the new transfer lemma
  `LocalCommutativity.commute_ι` (`LocalCommutativity.lean:106-117`,
  `lmm:local-commutativity-any-quasilocal`) proves commutation holds in *every*
  `QuasilocalAlgebra U i` — routing both witnesses through a common local algebra
  `𝔘(B)` via `QuasilocalAlgebra.commute_ι_iff_commute_map`. `Net.lean:213-234`
  (`commute_ι_of_spacelike`) instantiates this at `N.quasilocal`, and
  `EinsteinCausality.lean`, `LocalVonNeumann.lean` and `GeometricCovariance.lean`
  are now uniformly stated for representations `π : N.quasilocal.carrier →⋆ₐ[ℂ] …`
  — one coherent object, matching the blueprint's single "quasilocal algebra 𝔘"
  throughout `def:local-von-neumann` onward. `GeometricCovariance.lean` no longer
  has its own `covLocalOperators`/`covLocalVonNeumann`; it now uses
  `HaagKastlerNet.localOperators`/`localVonNeumann` from `LocalVonNeumann.lean`
  directly (confirmed by reading both files in full).
- **M1 (missing `Restriction:` marker) — fixed.** `PositiveEnergy.lean:49-53`
  (`IsPositiveEnergy`) and `VacuumState.lean:137-141, 198-199`

  `**Restriction:**` lines naming the bounded-generator restriction and what it is

  `QuasilocalKMS.lean:97-99` (`IsGroundStateForFlow`) carries the same marker,
  inheriting the restriction from `IsPositiveEnergy` as the docstring states.
