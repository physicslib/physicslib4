Detail on the two most structurally interesting items:

- **`GNS.gns_construction`/`gns_unique`.** The Lean theorem is stated over
  `[CStarAlgebra A]` (unital by Mathlib's definition) and produces
  `∃ (H : Type u) ..., IsCyclicVector π Ω ∧ (∀ a, ω a = ⟪Ω, π a Ω⟫) ∧ (ω.IsFaithful → Function.Injective π)`,
  built by instantiating Mathlib's own `PositiveLinearMap.GNS` machinery at
  `f := ω.toPositiveLinearMap`. This matches the blueprint's existential
  "one can construct a Hilbert space... and *-representation... such that..."
  clause-for-clause, including the faithfulness implication and the
  `π(a*) = π(a)^†` property (encoded by `π` being a `StarAlgHom` into
  `H →L[ℂ] H`, whose `star` is the Hilbert-space adjoint). `gns_unique` is
  stated as a free-standing uniqueness theorem over *any* two triples
  satisfying the cyclicity/reproducing-formula hypotheses (not literally "the
  triple built by `gns_construction`"), which matches the blueprint's own
  phrasing of uniqueness as a general fact about GNS triples.
- **`NetEquivalence`/`IsGenerallyCovariant`.** `NetEquivalence` is a
  `structure` with `theta` as a genuine field (not an existential witness),
  matching the blueprint's insistence that "Θ must be data"; its `naturality`
  field is stated against the two nets' `commIsotony` (Axiom 2) embeddings,
  which is now itself data carrying `map_self`/`map_comp` laws (verified by
  reading `Isotony.lean`, confirming the blueprint's claim that "Axiom 2 now
  carries the identity and composition laws itself" is actually true in the
  current Lean, not just asserted in prose). `IsGenerallyCovariant` wraps
  `NetEquivalence` in `Nonempty (...)`. This is a faithful reading, not a
  silent weakening: the blueprint's own text treats "the nets are equivalent"
  as an existence claim between two already-fixed nets (for each `(L, ψ)`
  independently), while `Θ`, once chosen, is data; the Lean encoding tracks
  exactly that distinction. `pullbackCarrierEquiv`/`pullbackCarrierEquiv_apply`
  confirm the relabelling bijection is literally `ψ` (not `ψ⁻¹`), matching the
  blueprint's explicit direction remark.

**L2 (new). Stale docstring remark in `Concrete.lean`, adjacent to the in-scope
general-covariance bridge, describing an issue that no longer exists in the
code it points to.** `Physicslib4/AQFT/HaagKastlerCurved/Concrete.lean:50-52`
says: *"One deferred refinement noted on `Spacetime.Isometry` still applies:
the bundled differential is not yet tied to the manifold derivative."* This
sentence sits in the module docstring for `toAbstract`/`toAbstractIdentityComponent`,
the exact bridge that `IsGenerallyCovariant`/`NetTheory` are stated over. But
`Physicslib4/Spacetime/Isometry.lean` (read in full, 200 lines) has no second,
separately-bundled notion of "differential" — its `Isometry` structure's
`preserves` field, and every theorem in the file
(`preserves_self`, `isTimelike_mfderiv_iff`, etc.), is stated directly in terms
of `mfderiv`, the genuine manifold derivative; there is no other "bundled
differential" construct anywhere in that file or in `IsometryTopology.lean`/
`IsometryCausality.lean` for such a remark to refer to (grep-verified: the
phrase and its keywords appear nowhere else in the repository). Either this
note is stale (the "deferred refinement" was resolved when `Isometry.preserves`
was written directly against `mfderiv`, and the comment was never deleted) or
it refers to something that has since been renamed/removed without updating
this cross-reference. Low severity — it is a documentation-accuracy issue, not
a statement or proof defect (`toAbstract`/`toAbstractIdentityComponent`
themselves are unaffected and match their own docstrings), but it risks
misleading a future reader into believing there is an open modelling gap in
the isometry group that `IsGenerallyCovariant`'s Axiom-5 hypothesis depends on.
Not found in any prior memo under this or a related description; this is a
new finding.

**L3 (checked, no issue found). `IsKMSState` does not itself require `α` to
satisfy `IsOneParameterAut`; the invariance theorems add it as an explicit
hypothesis.** `Physicslib4/AQFT/KMS.lean`'s `IsKMSState α β ω` is a predicate
over an arbitrary family `α : ℝ → (A ≃⋆ₐ[ℂ] A)`, with no built-in requirement
that `α` be additive/normalized, even though the blueprint's `def:kms-state`
reads "for a one-parameter automorphism group α". This looks at first like
"Lean assumes less", but on inspection it is a deliberate and consistently
applied modularity choice: the blueprint's own theorem statements that
actually need the group law (`thrm:kms-invariance` /
`IsKMSState.invariant_of_pos`) carry `hα : IsOneParameterAut α` as an explicit
hypothesis in both the `.tex` reading (definition of KMS state and the
one-parameter-group definition are separate blueprint nodes, `def:one-parameter-aut`
vs. `def:kms-state`) and the Lean statement, while the theorems that don't need
it (`convexCombo`, `correlationOne`, `correlation_eqOn`) don't carry it. No
mismatch; I record this only because it is exactly the shape of issue this
audit looks for and it is worth showing it was checked, not merely assumed
fine.

of foundational restriction that silently narrows everything downstream, so I
re-traced it end-to-end this session to confirm it still propagates correctly

`IsGeodesic` field) and found no place where the restriction is silently
dropped or re-strengthened without the marker.
