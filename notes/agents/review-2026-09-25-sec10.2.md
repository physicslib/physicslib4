### Low

2. **`thrm:gelfand-formula` states an unused, and unnecessary, hypothesis.**
   `blueprint/src/sections/sec10/spectral-theorems.tex:2686`: "Suppose
   $\mathbf{H} \ne \{0\}$ and $A \in \mathcal{B}(\mathbf{H})$." But:
   - the theorem's own `\uses{def:bounded-operator-notation,
     def:spectral-radius}` (line 2684) does **not** cite
     `conv:nonzero-hilbert-space` (the label the blueprint elsewhere uses for
     the standing $H \ne \{0\}$ convention — see the next theorem,
     `lmm:hall-8.1`, whose `\uses` at line 2706 does cite it);
   - the cited Mathlib lemma,
     `spectrum.pow_nnnorm_pow_one_div_tendsto_nhds_spectralRadius`
     (`Mathlib/Analysis/Normed/Algebra/GelfandFormula.lean:122`), is stated
     for an arbitrary complex Banach algebra `A` with **no** nontriviality
     hypothesis at all: `theorem pow_nnnorm_pow_one_div_tendsto_nhds_spectralRadius
     (a : A) : Tendsto (fun n : ℕ => (‖a ^ n‖₊ : ℝ≥0∞) ^ (1 / n : ℝ)) atTop (𝓝
     (spectralRadius ℂ a))`;
   - the theorem's own (one-line, `\leanok`-only) blueprint proof at line
     2691-2693 does not use the hypothesis either.
   This is a "blueprint states more than Lean needs" narrowing in the benign
   direction (Lean is strictly more general, matching the prior review's Low
   finding 4 about separability/nontriviality prose conventions), not a
   soundness problem — but it is a fresh instance introduced by the Gelfand
   swap, and worth cleaning up for consistency: either drop the hypothesis
   from the theorem statement, or add `conv:nonzero-hilbert-space` to its
   `\uses` for traceability. No Lean change needed.

3. **Representation gap (`ℝ≥0∞` vs. real number, and general Banach algebra
   vs. `𝓑(H)`) for `thrm:gelfand-formula` is not spelled out at its own
   citation site, unlike its neighbors.** `Physicslib4/Spectral/Spectrum.lean`
   lines 43-73 carry a "Blueprint nodes that are literally Mathlib
   declarations" section documenting the `ℝ≥0∞`-vs-real caveat for
   `def:spectral-radius`, `crllr:crllr-1`, and `lmm:hall-8.1`, but does not
   list `thrm:gelfand-formula` itself, even though the same caveat applies
   to it (the Lean statement's conclusion is `Tendsto ... (𝓝 (spectralRadius
   ℂ a))` in `ℝ≥0∞`, vs. the blueprint's real-valued
   `R(A) = \lim \|A^m\|^{1/m}`). The gap is harmless — `lmm:hall-8.1`'s
   entry, three lines below where `thrm:gelfand-formula` is used, makes the
   general pattern clear — but a reader auditing `thrm:gelfand-formula` in
   isolation has to reconstruct the correspondence themselves. Suggested:
   add one sentence to the `Spectrum.lean` module doc extending the
   bulleted list to `thrm:gelfand-formula`.

4. **(Carried forward, re-verified) `spectrum ℝ A` stands in for `σ(A) ⊆
   ℂ`.** Re-confirmed by reading `ContinuousCalculus.lean` and
   `SpectralTheorem.lean` implementation notes; still documented, still a
   deliberate and licensed (by `prpstn:hall-7.7`) representation choice, no
   action needed.

5. **(Carried forward, re-verified) `@[nolint unusedArguments]` on `L0`**
   (`Physicslib4/Spectral/BorelClasses.lean:279`) remains justified by an
   adjacent docstring; re-confirmed this is the only `nolint` in scope.
