## Findings

### R06-1. The Minkowski weak→strong continuity upgrade has no blueprint node
- severity: minor
- category: blueprint-lean-mismatch
- location: `Physicslib4/Analysis/StrongContinuity.lean:54` (`strongContinuous_of_weak`),
  `Physicslib4/AQFT/HaagKastler/QuasilocalIntertwiner.lean:584`
  (`IsInvariantState.exists_gns_unitary_strongContinuous`) and `:630`
  (`IsInvariantState.exists_gns_unitaryRep_strongContinuous`), compare
  `blueprint/src/sections/sec10/haag-kastler-axioms-in-curved-spacetime.tex:662`
- protected: no
- verdict: confirmed
- evidence: `Physicslib4/Analysis/StrongContinuity.lean`'s module docstring states its purpose
  explicitly: "the spacetime-agnostic ingredient behind the strong continuity of the GNS
  unitary `U(L)` attached to an invariant state: weak continuity of the matrix coefficients on
  the dense cyclic vectors yields strong continuity of `L ↦ U(L)ψ`". Searching the whole
  blueprint tree finds no `\lean{...strongContinuous_of_weak...}`,
  `...exists_gns_unitary_strongContinuous...}`, or
  `...exists_gns_unitaryRep_strongContinuous...}` tag anywhere:
  `grep -rn "strongContinuous_of_weak\|exists_gns_unitary_strongContinuous\|exists_gns_unitaryRep_strongContinuous\|exists_gns_unitary_of_invariant_strongContinuous" blueprint/src/`
  returns nothing. By contrast, the structurally parallel *curved*-spacetime declaration
  `Physicslib4.AQFT.HaagKastlerCurved.HaagKastlerNet.IsKMSStateForFlow.exists_gns_unitary_strongContinuous`
  *is* cited, at `haag-kastler-axioms-in-curved-spacetime.tex:662`. So the Minkowski-side
  weak-continuity upgrade — an entire file plus two public wrapper theorems
  (`IsInvariantState.exists_gns_unitary_strongContinuous`,
  `IsInvariantState.exists_gns_unitaryRep_strongContinuous`) — is formalized and used nowhere
  in the tracked blueprint statements, even though its curved-spacetime counterpart is. This
  does not misstate anything (the lemma is mathematically sound and the proof is self-contained
  Hilbert-space analysis), but it means `blueprint/src/sections/sec10/haag-kastler-axioms.tex`
  does not record a capability that exists in Lean, so a reader of the blueprint cannot find
  or check the intended statement for it, and `\uses{}`/`\lean{}` harmony cannot be audited for
  it at all.
- proposed fix: add a blueprint lemma (e.g. a sibling of `thrm:invariant-state-gns-unitary`)
  stating the weak-continuity hypothesis and the strong-continuity conclusion for the Minkowski
  covariant GNS representation, tagged with `\lean{Physicslib4.AQFT.HaagKastler.HaagKastlerNet.IsInvariantState.exists_gns_unitary_strongContinuous}`
  (and the bundled form), mirroring the curved-spacetime node already present.

### R06-2. One blueprint `\lean{}` tag names a lemma that does not state the blueprint claim
- severity: nit
- category: uses-tags
- location: `blueprint/src/sections/sec10/haag-kastler-axioms.tex:1936`
  (`\lean{Physicslib4.AQFT.generator_eq_of_eq_expUnitary, Physicslib4.AQFT.exp_generator_unique}`
  for `lmm:exp-generator-unique`), paired with `Physicslib4/AQFT/PositiveEnergy.lean:73-95`
- protected: no
- verdict: confirmed
- evidence: the blueprint lemma reads "Let `V(t)` ... be a family of unitary operators ...
  and let `B` be a self-adjoint operator with `V(t) = e^{itB}` for all `t`. Then `B` is the
  infinitesimal generator of `V` ...". `generator_eq_of_eq_expUnitary` is exactly this claim.
  The second name in the tag, `exp_generator_unique`, instead proves: for *bounded* `P, Q`, if
  `exp(itP) = exp(itQ)` for all `t, x` then `P = Q` — a different statement (equality of two
  bounded generators of the *same* family, not "B is the generator of V"), and its own
  docstring in `PositiveEnergy.lean:67-72` says "The unbounded form is
  `generator_eq_of_eq_expUnitary`", i.e. it self-identifies as an auxiliary bounded-case
  lemma rather than a direct translation of the blueprint prose.
- proposed fix: either drop `exp_generator_unique` from the `\lean{}` tag of
  `lmm:exp-generator-unique` (it is proof-internal scaffolding, not a restatement of the
  lemma), or add a sentence to the blueprint lemma noting the bounded uniqueness fact it
  depends on.
