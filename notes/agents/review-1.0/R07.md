## Findings

### R07-1. Unused `v`-timelike hypothesis in reverse Cauchy-Schwarz
- severity: nit
- category: statement-correctness
- location: Physicslib4/Spacetime/LorentzCauchySchwarz.lean:46-93 (theorem `reverse_cauchy_schwarz_of_lorentzianAt`)
- protected: no
- verdict: confirmed
- evidence: the hypothesis is declared `(_hv : B v v < 0)` (underscore name) and never appears in the
  proof body (only `hw`, `hL`, `hb` are used); the algebraic certificate `hkey`/`hwc0` only needs `w`
  timelike. The blueprint (`lmm:reverse-cauchy-schwarz`, spacetime.tex:67-78) states the hypothesis
  symmetrically ("let v, w be timelike") so the Lean statement matches the blueprint's *stated*
  hypotheses, but the proof reveals the true fact is strictly more general (only `w` timelike is
  needed) and the asymmetric hidden generality is undocumented.
- proposed fix: either drop the unused `_hv` and state the stronger lemma (noting in the docstring
  that only `w` need be timelike), or, if the symmetric statement is intentional for parity with the
  blueprint text, add a comment noting the hypothesis is unused by design to match the blueprint's
  phrasing.

### R07-2. The orthochronous-Lorentz-group section of Minkowski.lean has no blueprint node
- severity: major
- category: blueprint-lean-mismatch
- location: Physicslib4/Spacetime/Minkowski.lean:197-505 (`IsLorentz`, `IsProper`, `IsOrthochronous`,
  `isLorentz_refl/trans/symm`, `isProper_refl/trans/symm`, `isOrthochronous_refl/trans/symm`,
  `cauchySchwarz_sq_three`, `pos_of_reverse_cauchySchwarz`, `timelike_sameSign_of_minkowskiForm_neg`,
  `minkowskiForm_single_zero_left/right`, `minkowskiForm_smul_left/right`,
  `isOrthochronous_pos_time_of_timelike`)
- protected: no
- verdict: confirmed
- evidence: `grep -rn "IsLorentz\|IsProper\|IsOrthochronous" blueprint/src/` returns no matches
  anywhere in the blueprint (checked the full tree, not just the in-scope lines). The module
  docstring (Minkowski.lean:20-27) advertises only `StandardMinkowskiSpacetime`, `alexandrovTopology`
  and `MinkowskiSpacetime` as "the last three definitions of section 10.5"; it does not mention this
  ~300-line block on the orthochronous Lorentz group `SO(1,3)↑` at all, even though it sits between
  `StandardMinkowskiSpacetime` (blueprint `def:standard-minkowski-spacetime`, in scope) and the
  Alexandrov-topology material (blueprint lines 1073+, out of scope but present). CLAUDE.md states
  "The blueprint … is the specification" and "Lean is checked against it"; this block of proved
  mathematics (that `IsLorentz`/`IsProper`/`IsOrthochronous` form groupoid-like closure properties,
  and that orthochronous Lorentz maps preserve the sign of the time component of timelike vectors)
  is entirely unauditable against stated intent because no blueprint prose states what it is for.
- proposed fix: add a blueprint subsection (with `\lean{}`/`\uses{}` tags) stating the definitions of
  `IsLorentz`, `IsProper`, `IsOrthochronous` and the closure/future-cone-preservation facts, or, if
  this material is scaffolding for a later theorem not yet blueprinted, say so in the module
  docstring and update the "Main definitions" list.

### R07-3. `backward.isDefEq.respectTransparency false` on `StandardMinkowskiSpacetime` is unexplained
- severity: minor
- category: lean-style
- location: Physicslib4/Spacetime/Minkowski.lean:96 (`set_option backward.isDefEq.respectTransparency false in` immediately before `noncomputable def StandardMinkowskiSpacetime`)
- protected: no
- verdict: confirmed
- evidence: the option is set with no comment explaining why it is needed for this particular
  declaration (the surrounding docstring at lines 97-107 discusses only the mathematical content).
  The brief singles out this option (alongside `maxHeartbeats`) as needing justification because it
  changes elaborator unification behavior and can mask defeq-fragility that would resurface on a
  Mathlib bump.
- proposed fix: add a one-line comment at the `set_option` recording which field's elaboration needs
  the relaxed transparency and why (e.g. which `infer_instance`/`change` step fails without it).

### R07-4. LorentzCausality.lean is almost entirely absent from the blueprint
- severity: major
- category: blueprint-lean-mismatch
- location: Physicslib4/Spacetime/LorentzCausality.lean:1-489 (whole file; 24 declarations)
- protected: no
- verdict: confirmed
- evidence: the module docstring (lines 8-45) states the file "proves that the inhomogeneous
  Lorentz group acts on the causal vocabulary of standard Minkowski spacetime" and names its "Main
  results" as `isSpacelikeRelated_congr`, `isCompletelySpacelike_image`, `lorentzPath`,
  `causallyPrecedes_smul_iff`, `isSpacelikeRelated_smul_iff`, `isCompletelySpacelike_smul_iff`.
  Grepping each of the file's 24 top-level declaration names against
  `blueprint/src/sections/sec10/spacetime.tex` finds only one hit, `lorentzPath_isGeodesic`, which
  occurs once at line 1543 (out of this review's range) buried in an unrelated `\lean{}` list about
  isometry pushforwards (`Physicslib4.Spacetime.Isometry.pushforwardPath_isGeodesic,
  Physicslib4.lorentzPath_isGeodesic, Physicslib4.Spacetime.CrossIsometry.pushforwardPath_isGeodesic`).
  The file's own headline results — Lorentz-invariance of causal precedence
  (`causallyPrecedes_smul_iff`) and of complete spacelikeness (`isCompletelySpacelike_smul_iff`) —
  have no blueprint node anywhere in the repository. As with R07-2, this leaves a geometrically
  significant and substantial (489-line) piece of chapter-10 Lean content with no stated blueprint
  intent to check it against.
- proposed fix: add a blueprint subsection for Poincaré/Lorentz covariance of the causal relations
  (or point to wherever this is meant to land, e.g. a Haag-Kastler-axioms section on covariance) with
  `\lean{}` tags for at least the headline results named in the module docstring.

## Summary

- Counts by severity: major 2 (R07-2, R07-4), minor 1 (R07-3), nit 1 (R07-1). Counts by category:
  blueprint-lean-mismatch 2, statement-correctness 1, lean-style 1.

  1. R07-4 — LorentzCausality.lean (489 lines) essentially invisible to the blueprint.
  2. R07-2 — Minkowski.lean's orthochronous-Lorentz-group section (~300 lines) invisible to the
     blueprint.

  4. R07-1 — unused hypothesis signalling a statement narrower than the proof actually needs.
- Verified with tools (`grep`/`wc` over `blueprint/src` and the Lean sources, reading the actual
  declarations): all four findings. No build or `lean_diagnostic_messages` runs were used (not
  needed to confirm any of the above; all are textual/structural). No findings here rest on judgment
  alone without a grep/read confirmation.
- Cross-cutting pattern: two of the four findings (R07-2, R07-4) are the same shape — large blocks of
  proved Lean mathematics about Lorentz/Poincaré covariance of the causal structure that postdate (or
  were never folded into) the blueprint's coverage of chapter 10. A staged fix plan should treat
  "blueprint catch-up for Lorentz-covariance material" as one unit of work covering both files, rather
  than two independent patches.
