## Findings

### R11-1. Axiom 5's orientation-preserving restriction is undocumented in the blueprint and carries no `Restriction:` marker in Lean
- severity: minor
- category: stale-claim / lean-docstring (restriction nobody recorded)
- location: `Physicslib4/AQFT/HaagKastlerCurved/Concrete.lean:60-69` (`toAbstractIdentityComponent`, `Isom := ↥(Spacetime.Isometry.orientedIdentityComponent ...)`); blueprint `blueprint/src/sections/sec10/haag-kastler-axioms-in-curved-spacetime.tex:93-126` (`def:isometric-covariance-in-curved-spacetime`)
- protected: no
- verdict: confirmed
- evidence: The blueprint's Axiom 5 says only "A member φ of the group of isometries of M connected to the identity acts on 𝔘(B)...", with no mention of orientation. The concrete bridge instantiates the abstract interface's `Isom` field as the *oriented* identity component (identity component ∩ future-orientation-preserving), not the plain identity component. The only place this gap is explained is `home_page/index.md:77`: "'Isometries connected to the identity' and 'identity-component isometries preserving the future orientation' describe the same group, but the inclusion of the former in the latter rests on a Myers–Steenrod-type rigidity result that Mathlib does not provide. The Lean therefore intersects the identity component with the explicitly orientation-preserving subgroup." Neither the blueprint tex (which governs intent per `CLAUDE.md`) nor the Lean docstring of `toAbstractIdentityComponent` (or `IsometricCovariance`) states this restriction; `CLAUDE.md` requires deliberate restrictions to be recorded in the docstring on a line starting `**Restriction:**`, and no such marker exists anywhere in `Physicslib4/AQFT/HaagKastlerCurved/` (confirmed by grep). The home-page note is a reasonable account, but it is outside the blueprint and is not reproduced as a Lean-side `Restriction:` comment, so a reader of the blueprint alone, or of the Lean docstring alone, would not learn that the concrete instantiation is narrower than the stated axiom pending an unformalized rigidity theorem.
- proposed fix: add a `**Restriction:**` line to the docstring of `toAbstractIdentityComponent` (and/or `IsometricCovariance`/Axiom 5 definition in the blueprint) naming the Myers–Steenrod-type rigidity fact it is waiting on, mirroring the home-page note; optionally add a short remark to `def:isometric-covariance-in-curved-spacetime` or `def:lorentzian-spacetime` in the blueprint acknowledging the same gap for the concrete bridge.
