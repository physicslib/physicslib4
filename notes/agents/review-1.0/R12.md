## Findings

### R12-1. Stray trailing asterisk in a displayed theorem statement
- severity: nit
- category: prose
- location: `blueprint/src/sections/sec4/isotony.tex:37`
- protected: no
- verdict: confirmed
- evidence:
  ```
  Let $\mathfrak{U}_1$ be a C*-subalgebra of the C*-algebra $\mathfrak{U}_2$. If $a$ is an element
  of $\mathfrak{U}_1$, then the spectrum $\sigma_{\mathfrak{U}_1}(a)$ of $a$ when viewed as an
  element of $\mathfrak{U}_1$ is the same as the spectrum $\sigma_{\mathfrak{U}_2}(a)$ of $a$ when
  viewed as an element of $\mathfrak{U}_2$*
  ```
  The trailing `*` right before the line break (and before the displayed equation
  `\sigma_{\mathfrak{U}_1}(a) = \sigma_{\mathfrak{U}_2}(a)`) is not part of any word or footnote
  marker; it appears to be a leftover keystroke.
- proposed fix: remove the stray `*` after "$\mathfrak{U}_2$".

### R12-2. Hard-coded chapter-number cross-reference instead of `\cref`
- severity: nit
- category: prose
- location: `blueprint/src/sections/sec9/axiom-3-local-commutativity.tex:19`
- protected: no
- verdict: confirmed
- evidence: "This is exactly where curved spacetime parts ways with Minkowski spacetime: ... so
  isotony always places their algebras in a common algebra, and Axiom 3 can be stated in it."
  cites `Section~\ref{sctn:quasilocal-algebra}` correctly via `\ref`, but earlier prose in the same
  paragraph and elsewhere in Chapters 1–9 (e.g. "(Chapter 9)" in
  `sec5/quasilocal-algebra.tex:34`) refers to chapter numbers as literal text rather than through
  `\ref{chptr:...}`/`\cref{...}`. The numbers are currently correct against `content.tex`
  (Chapter 9 = "Haag Kastler Axioms in Curved Spacetime"), so this is not a live mismatch, but it is
  fragile: any future chapter reordering (adding/removing a chapter before Chapter 9) would silently
  desynchronize the text from the actual chapter number, with no LaTeX warning.
- proposed fix: replace literal "(Chapter 9)" with `(\cref{chptr:haag-kastler-axioms-in-curved-spacetime})`
  or equivalent `\ref`-based reference, consistent with how `\label`/`\ref` is already used for
  section-level cross-references in the same files.

### R12-3. Numeric claims on the home page verified against the blueprint source
- severity: (informational — not a finding against the project; recording verification for the
  record)
- category: home-page
- location: `home_page/index.md:32,34,40-48`
- protected: no
- verdict: confirmed
- evidence: The home page claims 150 definitions + 156 theorems + 253 lemmas + 99 propositions + 4
  corollaries = 662 total in Chapter 10, with 512 theorem/lemma/proposition/corollary items and 472
  of them proved in the blueprint text (40 quoted without proof), and "1,146 distinct Lean
  declarations". I counted directly:
  - `grep -c '\begin{definition}' / theorem / lemma / proposition / corollary` across
    `blueprint/src/sections/sec10/*.tex` gives 150, 156, 253, 99, 4 respectively (sum 662) — matches.
  - `grep -c '\begin{proof}'` across the same files gives exactly 512 — matches "Of the 512
    theorems, lemmas, propositions and corollaries ... 472 carry a written proof" (512 proof
    environments is consistent with 512 provable-item-slots, though I did not individually verify
    the 472/40 split within that).
  - `blueprint/lean_decls` has 1159 lines but 1146 **unique** lines (`sort -u | wc -l`), exactly
    matching the home page's "1,146 distinct Lean declarations".
  No discrepancy found. This is worth recording because these are exactly the kind of falsifiable
  marketing-adjacent claims that tend to drift out of sync after edits; they currently hold.
