## Findings

### R01-1. `thrm:gns-construction-theorem` proof's `\uses{}` edges are spurious: the Lean proof never calls `lmm1`, `lmm2`, or `cauchy_schwarz_inequality`
- severity: major
- category: uses-tags
- location: blueprint/src/sections/sec10/gns-construction-details.tex:58 (proof `\uses{def:state, def:cyclic-vector, lmm:cauchy-schwarz-inequality, lmm:lmm1, lmm:lmm2}`); Physicslib4/GNS/Construction.lean:64-150 (`gns_construction`)
- protected: no
- verdict: confirmed
- evidence: the blueprint proof's `\uses` line at tex:58 cites `lmm:cauchy-schwarz-inequality, lmm:lmm1, lmm:lmm2`, and the surrounding prose (tex:61-324) walks through building `H_ω` as a quotient of `A` by the null space `N`, using Cauchy-Schwarz, `lmm1` (`N = N₁`) and `lmm2` (closedness/linearity of `N`) along the way. But `Physicslib4.GNS.gns_construction` instead sets `f := ω.toPositiveLinearMap` and builds the triple directly from Mathlib's pre-existing GNS machinery (`f.GNS`, `f.gnsStarAlgHom`, `f.toPreGNS`, `PositiveLinearMap.gnsStarAlgHom_apply`, etc., from `Mathlib.Analysis.CStarAlgebra.GelfandNaimarkSegal`). `grep -n "cauchy_schwarz_inequality\|lmm1\|lmm2\|nullSet\|nullSubmodule\|orthSet" Physicslib4/GNS/Construction.lean` returns no matches: none of the three cited results, nor the `nullSet`/`nullSubmodule` they are about, appear anywhere in the file. `home_page/sec10-1-gns.md:12` reinforces the same (now inaccurate) claim: "States and proves the GNS Construction Theorem ... in full detail—construction of the GNS Hilbert space ... since both the theorem and specific steps of its proof are used in the axioms that follow," and lines 28-32 list Lemmas 16-18 (Cauchy-Schwarz, `lmm1`, `lmm2`) under "Auxiliary Results Used in the Proof."
- proposed fix: either (a) correct the `\uses{}` edge to drop `lmm:cauchy-schwarz-inequality, lmm:lmm1, lmm:lmm2` (and instead note that `gns_construction` is discharged by Mathlib's GNS construction, with a `\uses{}`-style reference to that fact if the blueprint wants to record it), and soften the home-page claim that "specific steps of its proof are used in the axioms that follow," or (b) rewrite the proof of `gns_construction` to actually go through `nullSet`/`nullSubmodule`/`cauchy_schwarz_inequality` as narrated, if the project wants the formalization to track the hand-written proof rather than delegate to Mathlib. Either way the blueprint's five-part narrated proof (construction of `H_ω`, of `π_ω`, of `Ω`, faithfulness, uniqueness) does not correspond to how `gns_construction` is actually proved, which readers of the blueprint alongside the Lean file would find surprising.

### R01-2. `lmm1`/`lmm2`/`cauchy_schwarz_inequality` are proved but unused by the theorem they were introduced to support
- severity: minor
- category: lean-structure
- location: Physicslib4/GNS/NullSpace.lean:63-166; Physicslib4/GNS/CauchySchwarz.lean:83-187; Physicslib4/GNS/Construction.lean:64-150
- protected: no
- verdict: confirmed
- evidence: NullSpace.lean's module docstring (lines 8-31) and CauchySchwarz.lean's module docstring (lines 9-38) both introduce their results explicitly as the "detour" lemmas needed for the GNS construction proof (matching tex:86-92, "before being able to see this plan through we'll need to take a quick detour..."), yet `Construction.lean`'s actual proof of `gns_construction` (see R01-1) never calls them. (They are reported used elsewhere in the repo — `Physicslib4/GNS/ExtremeState.lean` and `Physicslib4/GNS/RadonNikodym.lean` — but not by the theorem the blueprint says they support.)
- proposed fix: same as R01-1; this is the Lean-side symptom of the same root cause. If the decision is to keep delegating to Mathlib's GNS construction, add a note to `Construction.lean`'s docstring clarifying that `lmm1`/`lmm2`/`cauchy_schwarz_inequality` are independent results used elsewhere (RadonNikodym/ExtremeState) rather than feeding into `gns_construction`.

### R01-3. `nullSubmodule_isClosed` duplicates `lmm2`'s content with no stated difference
- severity: nit
- category: lean-style
- location: Physicslib4/GNS/NullSpace.lean:142-166
- protected: no
- verdict: plausible
- evidence: `nullSubmodule_isClosed` (line 142-153) proves exactly the closedness clause that `lmm2` (line 163-165) re-derives by calling it: `theorem lmm2 ... := ⟨nullSubmodule ω, nullSubmodule_coe ω, nullSubmodule_isClosed ω⟩`. The module docstring (line 29-30) explains the split as deliberate ("The primary entry point is `lmm2`; the helper `nullSubmodule_isClosed` records closedness separately for convenience"), so this is not clearly a defect, but the helper is only used once (inside `lmm2` itself) and adds a second public-facing name for the same fact.
- proposed fix: none required; note only if the project wants to trim the public API surface.

### R01-4. `Basic.lean`'s docstring claims `def:cyclic-vector` is kept "general enough" for a plain algebra, but `IsCyclicVector` actually requires a full `CStarAlgebra` instance
- severity: minor
- category: lean-docstring
- location: Physicslib4/GNS/Basic.lean:36-39 (docstring), :47 (`variable (A : Type*) [CStarAlgebra A]`), :116-117 (`IsCyclicVector`); blueprint/src/sections/sec10/gns-construction-details.tex:26-36 (`def:cyclic-vector`)
- protected: no
- verdict: confirmed
- evidence: the blueprint definition says "Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an algebra represented by the bounded operators $\pi(\mathcal{A})$..." — no C*-structure is required of $\mathcal{A}$. The module docstring explicitly claims: "The blueprint statement of `def:cyclic-vector` only requires `A` to be an algebra; here we keep the assumptions general enough to be reusable but require the operator-algebra side `H →L[ℂ] H` to make sense, so `H` is a complex Hilbert space." But the file's `variable (A : Type*) [CStarAlgebra A]` at line 47 is never closed or weakened before `IsCyclicVector` is declared (lines 106-117 only change `A`'s explicit/implicit binder, not its instances), so `Physicslib4.GNS.IsCyclicVector` is defined only for `A` with a full `CStarAlgebra A` instance (norm, completeness, *-structure compatible with the norm) — strictly more than "an algebra." In the project's own practice `A` is always instantiated as a `CStarAlgebra` at every call site, so no theorem is currently narrowed by this, but the docstring's claim of generality on the `A` side is false as written.
- proposed fix: either state `IsCyclicVector` for a bare (unital) algebra `A` with a `*`-representation into `H →L[ℂ] H` (dropping `CStarAlgebra A` and taking the representation as a plain `AlgHom`/ring hom rather than `A →⋆ₐ[ℂ] (H →L[ℂ] H)`, if that is wanted for reuse outside the C*-algebra setting), or correct the docstring to say the generality is kept only on the `H` side and that `A` is fixed to a `CStarAlgebra` to match every actual use site.

### R01-5. `isCompact_weakStateSet` has an unrecorded `[Nontrivial A]` restriction absent from the blueprint statement
- severity: minor
- category: statement-correctness
- location: Physicslib4/GNS/PureStateExists.lean:83-92 (`isCompact_weakStateSet`); blueprint/src/sections/sec10/haag-kastler-axioms.tex:1331-1343 (`thrm:state-space-weak-compact`)
- protected: no
- verdict: confirmed
- evidence: the blueprint theorem states unconditionally, "The state space, realized inside the weak-* dual $\mathrm{WeakDual}\,\mathbb{C}\,A$ ..., is weak-* compact," for "a unital C*-algebra" (no nontriviality hypothesis, and Mathlib's `CStarAlgebra` class, `Classes.lean:38-39`, does not itself require `Nontrivial A`). But the Lean statement is `theorem isCompact_weakStateSet [Nontrivial A] : IsCompact (weakStateSet : Set (WeakDual ℂ A))`. The extra hypothesis is not needed for the mathematical content — if `A` is the trivial ring (`1 = 0`), `weakStateSet` is simply empty (`φ 1 = 1` is unsatisfiable when `1 = 0` forces `φ 1 = 0`), hence trivially compact — but it is needed by the current proof because it rewrites through `norm_eq_re_apply_one_of_positive` (`Physicslib4/GNS/ExtremeState.lean:72`), which itself carries `[Nontrivial A]` as an explicit hypothesis rather than deriving it from positivity+normalization the way `State.apply_one` does (`ExtremeState.lean:110-111`, via `haveI := nontrivial_of_state ω`). No `**Restriction:**` docstring line records this gap, and the blueprint node is marked `\leanok`.
- proposed fix: either prove the `A` trivial case separately (`weakStateSet` is empty, hence compact) and drop `[Nontrivial A]` from `isCompact_weakStateSet`, or add a `**Restriction:**` line to its docstring naming the gap and noting it is waiting on generalizing `norm_eq_re_apply_one_of_positive` to derive nontriviality from its own hypotheses as `State.apply_one` does.
