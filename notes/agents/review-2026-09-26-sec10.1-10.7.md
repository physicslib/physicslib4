## New material reviewed in depth (per task's request)

- **`NetEquivalence` / `IsGenerallyCovariant` against the blueprint's notion.**
  Re-derived by reading, not just re-checked: `NetEquivalence` is a `structure`
  (the `Θ` family is a field, `theta`, not an existence witness — matches "Θ
  must be data"), and its `naturality` field is a `∀`-quantified equation over
  the *chosen* `commIsotony` embeddings of both nets — matches "the ιᵢ come
  for free" from Axiom 2. `IsGenerallyCovariant` wraps `NetEquivalence` in
  `Nonempty (...)`: this is correct, not a weakening — the blueprint's own
  prose treats "the nets are equivalent" as a relation between two already-given
  nets (an existence claim: *some* Θ witnesses it), while `Θ` itself, once
  chosen, is data. The two readings (data vs. existence) apply at different
  points in the blueprint text and the Lean encoding tracks the distinction
  correctly.
- **Direction of pullbacks.** Tool/read-verified (see Method):
  `pullbackCarrierEquiv : (L.pullback ψ).Carrier ≃ L.Carrier` and
  `pullback_alexandrovBasis_image` both run from the pullback spacetime to the
  original, i.e. `e := ψ` reads *from* `ψ*L` *to* `L`, exactly the direction
  the blueprint insists on ("it is ψ, not ψ⁻¹, that carries ψ*L-diamonds to
  L-diamonds", `general-covariance-in-curved-spacetime.tex:51`). No
  direction bug found.
- **GNS Lean types vs. blueprint objects.** `State A` (`A →L[ℂ] ℂ` + positivity
  + `‖·‖ = 1`) matches "element of the dual space that is positive and
  normalized" for `[CStarAlgebra A]` (a unital C*-algebra). `IsCyclicVector`
  matches (`Dense (Set.range fun a => π a Ω)`). `gns_construction`'s
  conclusion existentially quantifies `H : Type u` in `A`'s own universe
  (documented in the module docstring as a deliberate universe-polymorphism
  choice, not a mathematical restriction) and bundles cyclicity, the
  reproducing formula, and the faithfulness implication in one existential;
  `gns_unique` is the separate uniqueness-up-to-unitary-equivalence statement.
  Both match the blueprint theorem's content once read together (as the
  `\lean{}` tag now lists both, fixed in an earlier session).
- **Edge cases (trivial algebras / zero Hilbert space).** No blueprint
  divergence found. `State A` requires `[CStarAlgebra A]` with `‖ω‖ = 1`,
  which is unsatisfiable for the zero ring (where `1 = 0` forces `‖1‖ = 0`);
  Mathlib's C*-algebra axioms already exclude the trivial algebra, so `State`
  is vacuously never invoked there — consistent with the blueprint, which
  never discusses that case either. `IsCyclicVector`/`gns_construction` place
  no constraint excluding the zero Hilbert space as such, but a `CStarAlgebra`
  is automatically nontrivial, so the constructed `H_ω` (built from `A`
  itself) cannot degenerate to the zero space either. Nothing here narrows or
  widens the blueprint's claim; not investigated further given the budget.
- **`GeometricCovariance.lean`'s `g • B₁ ⊆ B` change** (the specific fix named
  in the task). Read the whole diff (`git show 15b3ce6 --
  Physicslib4/AQFT/HaagKastlerCurved/GeometricCovariance.lean`): every
  occurrence of the hypothesis `h₁' : (g : M.Isom) • B₁ ⊆ B` was removed and
  replaced by the new lemma `smul_subset_of_mem_stabilizer g h₁ : (g : M.Isom)
  • B₁ ⊆ B` (derived from `h₁ : B₁ ⊆ B` and `g ∈ Stab(B)` via
  `Set.smul_set_mono` and `MulAction.mem_stabilizer_iff`). This is a genuine
  strengthening (one fewer hypothesis on `stabAutHom_commIsotony`,
  `lieConj_image_localOperators`, `lieConj_image_localVonNeumann`,
  `lieConj_image_localVonNeumannAlgebra`, `localVonNeumann_isFactor_smul`,
  `localVonNeumannEquiv`), reused consistently (grep-verified, single
  definition site, no duplication), and correctly reflected in the updated
  blueprint prose (`haag-kastler-axioms-in-curved-spacetime.tex:277`: "The
  inclusion g·B₁⊆B is not a hypothesis: it follows from B₁⊆B and g·B=B").
  No issues found.
