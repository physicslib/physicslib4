### Low

1. **New `Spectrum.lean` note on `thrm:gelfand-formula` is directionally correct but
   its edge-case phrasing overstates what is known.**
   `Physicslib4/Spectral/Spectrum.lean:74-80` (added in commit `15b3ce6`): "the
   blueprint keeps that hypothesis [`H ≠ {0}`] because its real-valued `R(A)`, a
   supremum over `σ(A) = ∅`, is not `0` on the zero space, whereas the limit is."
   The blueprint's own `def:spectral-radius` (`spectral-theorems.tex:2542-2545`)
   itself carries `\uses{conv:nonzero-hilbert-space, ...}`, i.e. `R(A)` is *undefined*
   at `H = {0}` in the blueprint's own vocabulary, not a defined quantity that happens
   to differ from `0`. The intended point — matching the adjacent, unchanged note on
   `def:spectral-radius` three lines above ("Mathlib's `⨆` over the empty set is `0`,
   ... rather than `-∞`") — is almost certainly "under the usual sup-of-the-empty-set
   convention this would read `-∞`, not `0`", but as written it reads as though `R(A)`
   is a well-defined nonzero real at `H = {0}`, which it is not per the blueprint's own
   hypotheses. This is prose-only (the cited Lean identifier,
   `spectrum.pow_nnnorm_pow_one_div_tendsto_nhds_spectralRadius`, is an exact,
   `leanok`-verified citation of the general Mathlib theorem and needs no change); the
   note would be more accurate reworded along the lines of "the blueprint's `R(A)` is
   undefined there (see the `def:spectral-radius` note above), whereas Mathlib's
   `ℝ≥0∞`-valued limit is well-defined and reads `0`." Judged by reading; the
   underlying Lean statement itself is unaffected and correct.
   **Location:** `Physicslib4/Spectral/Spectrum.lean:74-80`.

2. **(Carried forward, re-verified, still open) `thrm:gelfand-formula` states an
   unused, and Lean-unnecessary, `H ≠ {0}` hypothesis, and its own `\uses` does not

   `H ≠ {0}`" but the theorem's `\uses` at line 2684 lists only
   `def:bounded-operator-notation, def:spectral-radius`, and neither the one-line

   (drop the hypothesis, or add `conv:nonzero-hilbert-space` to `\uses` for

5. **Mildly confusing (but not incorrect) docstring wording on
   `existsUnique_assocMeasure`.** `ContinuousCalculus.lean:427-428`: "Existence is
   `assocMeasure` together with `integral_assocMeasure`; only uniqueness — which is
   the regularity half of Riesz–Markov–Kakutani — is left open." Read in isolation
   this suggests an unfinished proof, but the theorem immediately below is a complete,
   `sorry`-free `∃!` proof of *both* halves (uniqueness is discharged via
   `Measure.ext_of_integral_eq_on_compactlySupported`). "Is left open" appears to mean
   "is the one part not already packaged by a named Mathlib lemma", not "is
   unproved". Cosmetic; verified by reading the full proof body (no `sorry`) and by
   the clean `#print axioms` result on downstream capstone theorems that depend on it
   transitively.
