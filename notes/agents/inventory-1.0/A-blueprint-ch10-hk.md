## To CHANGELOG

- Axiom 2 (Minkowski and curved) was once stated with strict inclusion $\subset$. It now uses $\subseteq$, matching the Lean, so the identity law (b) is expressible.
- Axiom 2 gained the identity and composition laws as chosen data. Previously the isotony embeddings used downstream were witnesses chosen existentially inside Axiom 3, which had no composition law.
- Axiom 3 (Minkowski and curved) used to introduce its own isotony family and injectivity. It now only asserts commutation using the Axiom 2 family. The per-site coherence hypotheses were removed, for example from curved von Neumann isotony, the curved von Neumann net and net equivalence.
