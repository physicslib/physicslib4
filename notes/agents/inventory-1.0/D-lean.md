## To CHANGELOG

- Minkowski Axiom 3 was formerly stated as commutation in some quasilocal algebra; it is now stated in a common containing diamond, with `localCommutativity_iff_exists_commute_ι` proving the two forms equivalent (from `LocalCommutativity.lean:43,109`).
- `HaagKastlerNet` formerly bundled Axiom 4 as quasilocal completeness, `QuasilocalCompleteness := Nonempty (QuasilocalAlgebra U i)`, defined in `QuasilocalObservable.lean`. That predicate was removed once `exists_quasilocalAlgebra` (`thrm:quasilocal-algebra-exists`) proved the existence of the quasilocal algebra from the net alone. Axiom 4 is now the bridge principle in `ObservableBridge.lean` (from `Net.lean:31,127`, `QuasilocalObservable.lean:30`, `ObservableBridge.lean:35`).
- `QuasilocalAlgebra` formerly carried its own `inclusion` family, duplicating Axiom 2's; it is now parametrised by the Axiom 2 `Isotony` datum, and `trivialQuasilocalAlgebra` was re-indexed accordingly (from `QuasilocalAlgebra.lean:150`, `Net.lean:317,332`).
- `QuasilocalAlgebra.carrier` and `HaagKastlerNet` were made universe-polymorphic (from `Net.lean:70`).
- Curved spacetime: `commIsotony` was formerly chosen by `Classical.choose` from the Axiom 3 witness, with no composition law, so `localVonNeumann_mono` and other three-fold-inclusion sites carried a coherence hypothesis `hcoh`. It is now the Axiom 2 family, so `commIsotony_comp` holds for every net (from `HaagKastlerCurved/Net.lean:159,182`, `HaagKastlerCurved/LocalVonNeumann.lean:80`).
- The `isBasisSet_smul` field was added to the abstract curved `LorentzianSpacetime` interface, so basis-set preservation is no longer an explicit hypothesis in the curved geometric-covariance results (from `HaagKastlerCurved/GeometricCovariance.lean:22`).
- The `Spacetime` metric smoothness and `TimeOrientation.smooth` fields moved from a chart-local (`ContDiffWithinAt`, double-`mfderiv`) form to Mathlib's bundle-section idiom; the blueprint label `lmm:pullback-metric-smooth-in-charts` keeps its old name (from `Spacetime/Basic.lean:170`, `CausalStructure.lean:172`, `Pullback.lean:163`).
- The strip-Liouville principle in `KMS.lean` was first an explicit hypothesis and is now proved for `β > 0` (`stripLiouville_of_pos`), which makes KMS invariance unconditional (from `KMS.lean:38,81,222`).
- The half-plane topology helpers moved from `StripPeriodicExtension.lean` to `HorizontalLineRemovable.lean` (from `StripPeriodicExtension.lean:55`).
- The version-dated Mathlib remarks were written against `v4.31.0-rc1`; the project now pins v4.34.1 (from `LocalAlgebras.lean:92`, `QuasilocalAlgebra.lean:32`, `Spacetime/Basic.lean:35`).
