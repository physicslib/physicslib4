## To CHANGELOG

- spacetime.tex:1760 — the label `lmm:pullback-metric-smooth-in-charts` dates from when the smoothness field of `def:spacetime` was a chart-local `ContDiffWithinAt` condition; the label was kept because `thrm:pullback-is-spacetime` cites it.
- spacetime.tex:1823 — the smoothness fields of `Spacetime` and `TimeOrientation` were switched from a chart-local formulation to the bundle-section (`ContMDiff` section) form; this removed the accepted `tangentCoordChange` round-trip obligation from the time-orientation chain.
- spacetime.tex:1776/1784 — the Mathlib route for smoothness of `x ↦ dψ_x` (pullback metric) was an open API question, later settled via `ContMDiffAt.mfderiv_const` and `clm_precomp`/`clm_comp`; an earlier note wrongly claimed `ContMDiff.clm_comp` was Mathlib's only precomposition lemma (retraction).
- spacetime.tex:1638 — the `mfderiv`/`symm` round-trip cancellation was left implicit, then split out as `lmm:mfderiv-symm-cancel-left` / `-right`.
- spacetime.tex:1963 — `lmm:cross-metric-isometry-symm` was added to replace a hand-wave in the reverse inclusions of the chronological-future/past image lemmas.
- spectral-theorems.tex:93 — `def:identity-and-indicator` was split into `def:identity-operator` and `def:indicator-function`; the old anchor is kept as a compound reference.
- unbounded-spectral-theorems.tex:80 — Hall's Theorem A.52 was promoted to a citable result (`thrm:hall-a.52`) in the bounded spectral section.
