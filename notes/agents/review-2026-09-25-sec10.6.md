## New findings

### Medium

**N-1. The blueprint's claim that basis-set preservation is "discharged for a
net from a concrete geometric spacetime" is not backed by any Lean
declaration for the theorem it is attached to.**

- `haag-kastler-axioms-in-curved-spacetime.tex:281`
  (`thrm:von-neumann-geometric-covariance-in-curved-spacetime`): "the abstract
  `LorentzianSpacetime` interface does not supply basis-set preservation
  `M.IsBasisSet(g · B₁)`, so it enters as an explicit hypothesis, **discharged
  for a net from a concrete geometric spacetime**." (This clause predates
  today's edits — it was already present before `hcompat` was dropped — and
  was carried over unchanged.)
- No such discharge exists. `grep -rl` across the repository for
  `stabAutHom_commIsotony`, `lieConj_image_localOperators`,
  `lieConj_image_localVonNeumann`, `lieConj_image_localVonNeumannAlgebra`,
  `localVonNeumann_isFactor_smul`, `localVonNeumannEquiv` finds occurrences
  only in `GeometricCovariance.lean` itself — no `_geometric` or
  `_identityComponent` corollary anywhere, unlike the sibling axiom families
  in the same chapter that *do* have this pattern:
  `commute_of_spacelike_mono_geometric` (`Concrete.lean:96-108`),
  `commute_of_spacelike_mono_identityComponent` (`IdentityComponent.lean:153-167`),
  and `localVonNeumannAlgebra_le_commutant_of_subset_spacelikeComplement_geometric`
  (`Concrete.lean:117-128`).
- The ingredient needed to write such a corollary already exists:
  `Spacetime.LorentzianSpacetime.toAbstractIdentityComponent_isBasisSet_smul`
  (`IdentityComponent.lean:102-109`) proves exactly
  `M.IsBasisSet B → M.IsBasisSet (φ • B)` for `φ` an identity-component
  isometry over a concrete spacetime — precisely `hgB₁`. It just has not been
  applied to `GeometricCovariance.lean`'s theorem family the way it (or its
  sibling `isCompletelySpacelike_mono`) was applied to the other two families.
- *Suggested fix (report only)*: either add
  `lieConj_image_localVonNeumann_identityComponent` (etc.) corollaries to
  `IdentityComponent.lean` discharging `hgB₁` via
  `toAbstractIdentityComponent_isBasisSet_smul`, matching the existing
  pattern, or soften the blueprint prose to stop claiming a discharge that
  does not exist yet.

**N-2. Every theorem in `GeometricCovariance.lean` carries a hypothesis,
`h₁' : (g:M.Isom) • B₁ ⊆ B`, that is fully derivable from its neighbors and
does not need to be asked for.**

- Affected declarations: `stabAutHom_commIsotony`, `lieConj_image_localOperators`,
  `lieConj_image_localVonNeumann`, `lieConj_image_localVonNeumannAlgebra`,
  `localVonNeumann_isFactor_smul`, `localVonNeumannEquiv`
  (`GeometricCovariance.lean:50-166`) — six of six declarations in the file.
- Given `g : ↥(MulAction.stabilizer M.Isom B)` and `h₁ : B₁ ⊆ B`,
  `h₁' : (g:M.Isom) • B₁ ⊆ B` follows in one line:
  `MulAction.mem_stabilizer_iff.mp g.2 : g • B = B`, then
  `Set.smul_set_mono h₁ : g • B₁ ⊆ g • B`, so `g • B₁ ⊆ B`. I checked this
  compiles as a standalone lemma against the project's imports (tool-verified,
  see Method). The proof of `stabAutHom_commIsotony` itself already performs
  exactly this derivation internally, at line 57-60, via `e` and
  `Set.smul_set_mono h₁` — but for a *different* purpose (transporting
  `covEquiv_commIsotony`'s target), and the redundant `h₁'` is still taken as
  a separate explicit argument rather than derived once inside each
  declaration or factored into a `have`.
- This does not narrow what the theorems prove (the hypothesis is a tautology
  given `h₁` and `g`, so it costs nothing to satisfy), so it is not a
  faithfulness gap and I have not reclassified any statement-audit row over
  it. But it is exactly the kind of avoidable per-site hypothesis that this
  chapter's Axiom 2 rewrite (composition law `(c)`, blueprint `tex:45-46,
  226-228`) was designed to eliminate "throughout this chapter" — it was
  eliminated for the isotony/local-commutativity family and reintroduced,
  unnecessarily, in the newer geometric-covariance family.
- *Suggested fix*: drop `h₁'` from all six signatures and derive it internally
  from `h₁` and `g.2` (one `have`), the same way `hgB` already is at
  `stabAutHom_commIsotony:57-58`.
